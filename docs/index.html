How it's built

A small, reproducible ELT pipeline. One command rebuilds everything, and 28 dbt tests guard the numbers.

ExtractOur World in Data energy CSV (Ember + Energy Institute), 23k rows
LoadPython loads it untouched into DuckDB's raw schema
Transformdbt staging views: rename, filter, unpivot to long format
ModelStar schema: 3 dimensions, 2 facts, 28 data tests
ServePower BI report with DAX measures, and this page
Data: Our World in Data energy dataset, based on Ember and the Energy Institute Statistical Review. Shares are of total generation. Net imports above zero mean Germany bought more power than it sold.
